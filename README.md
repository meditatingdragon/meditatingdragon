### Featured Project: Intelligent Manuscript Editor

> **A distraction-free manuscript authoring platform with interactive worldbuilding and privacy-first background narrative intelligence.**

Key architectural highlights:

- **Rich Prose Authoring**: Distraction-free TipTap canvas, custom typography, debounced autosave, local cache synchronization, scene workflow tracking, and full snapshot undo/redo.
- **Document Ingestion & Snip Studio**: Asynchronous multi-format ingestion (`.docx`, `.odt`, `.md`, `.rtf`, `.txt`) with interactive dual-pane outline manipulation, cut dividers, and drag-and-drop scene reordering.
- **Story Bible & Knowledge Graph**: Centralized worldbuilding repository for characters, locations, factions, and artifacts with alias management and interactive **D3 force-directed narrative graphs**.
- **Background AI Narrative Intelligence**:
  - Asynchronous per-scene entity extraction powered by local **Ollama** LLMs.
  - Human-in-the-loop review staging tray (Approve, Merge as Alias, or Dismiss) before data enters the permanent graph.
  - Live non-blocking token-streaming assistant sidebar for writing inspiration.
- **Author Privacy & Safety by Design**: Strict scene-level and profile-level AI Shields guaranteeing sensitive user prose is never transmitted to untrusted external workers.
